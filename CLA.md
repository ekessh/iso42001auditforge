# Contributor License Agreement

The Licensor must hold sufficient rights to relicense AuditForge under
Apache-2.0 on the Change Date. To preserve that path, contributors must
sign off every commit.

## Individual Contributors — Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) 1.1

By making a contribution, you certify the DCO 1.1 statements
(https://developercertificate.org/). Sign off every commit:

```bash
git commit -s
```

The DCO bot blocks PRs lacking a `Signed-off-by:` trailer.

## Corporate Contributors — Corporate CLA

Companies contributing on behalf of employees must sign the corporate CLA
before merge. Contact: cla@auditforge.example.

## Why This Matters

BUSL-1.1 grants users limited rights and ultimately converts to Apache-2.0
on the Change Date. The DCO + corporate CLA combination preserves the
Licensor's ability to make that conversion (and any future relicensing
that benefits the user community) without re-collecting permissions.
